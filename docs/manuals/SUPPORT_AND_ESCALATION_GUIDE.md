# Support and Escalation Guide

## Contact matrix (complete before launch)
| Concern | First owner | Escalation | Target response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal usage/report help | [Service desk] | [System owner] | [SLA] |
| Claim/ownership dispute | [Responsible student/admin office] | [University authority] | [SLA] |
| Privacy/data request | [Data controller contact] | [DPO/legal authority] | [statutory/SLA] |
| Accessibility | [Accessibility owner] | [University support authority] | [SLA] |
| Security vulnerability | [Security contact] | [Incident commander] | Immediate triage |
| Provider/deployment outage | [Technical operator] | [Platform owner] | [SLA] |

## Ticket contents
Include contact method, report/claim ID, time, page and a privacy-safe description. Never send passwords, access tokens, full card data or unnecessary private evidence by ordinary email.

## Severity
- **P1:** active data exposure, account takeover, destructive corruption, public safety risk.
- **P2:** major workflow/provider outage with no safe workaround.
- **P3:** limited feature failure, accessibility barrier or delayed processing.
- **P4:** question, cosmetic issue or feature request.

P1 follows the incident-response plan immediately. Ownership disputes remain human decisions and may require in-person university verification.

# Student/User Manual

## 1. Sign in and profile
Register with an authorised email, verify it, sign in and complete student identifier/phone fields required for safe contact. Registration, login, forgot/reset password and email-verification screens—including validation, fallback messages and password visibility controls—follow the selected English, Sinhala or Tamil interface language. Profile information, avatar processing and password-change validation use the same language contract. Use Forgot Password when needed. Never share verification/reset links.

## 2. Public information and support
The About, Contact and legacy Lost/Found directory pages follow the selected English, Sinhala or Tamil language. Contact details are loaded only from approved public settings; unconfigured office, email or telephone fields are clearly marked rather than invented. Platform feedback requires sign-in and is persisted through the backend.

## 3. Search
Open **Search Items**, choose Lost/Found/Both, describe item/place/colour/date/feature, add category/date filters and switch list/grid view. Results show totals and load-more pagination. Active filters are reflected in the page URL for reload/share support. You may save up to five searches in the current browser for 30 days, rerun or delete them; avoid private identifiers in search text. A similarity score is not proof.

## 4. Assistant conversations
The Smart L&F assistant interface is available in English, Sinhala and Tamil, including result labels, report-draft review, account summaries, voice controls and privacy notices. It provides **New conversation** and **Conversation history** controls. History is text-only, stored in the current browser for up to seven days and limited to five recent sessions. Structured report cards, account summaries, images and claim evidence are not retained in assistant history. Delete one conversation or clear all local history at any time.

## 5. Report a lost or found item
Use the four-step wizard in English, Sinhala or Tamil: (1) photo and reviewable AI suggestions, (2) item details/unique features, (3) location/date and safe storage for found property, (4) privacy/review. Newly selected images can be rotated or centre-cropped in the browser; every edit triggers a fresh privacy review. Draft text autosaves. Existing reports use the same wizard. When offline, continue editing text and reconnect before submission; selected images cannot be restored after a reload. Multipart submission displays real upload progress, and low-data browser settings use stronger image compression. Review quality and duplicate warnings before submission.

## 6. Image privacy
Upload a clear image of the physical item. Do not include identity/payment cards, passwords, private documents or unrelated people. Every newly selected public photo is reviewed separately. If sensitive regions are detected, choose **Create privacy-safe copy** so the browser pixelates those regions and replaces the selected upload; the original browser file is not submitted. If automated review is unavailable, complete the explicit manual checklist or remove the photo before continuing. Existing private claim evidence follows the separate authorised-evidence workflow.

## 7. Manage reports and matches
Open **My Lost Reports** and **My Found Listings** to review, edit, delete or send feedback about your own records. Headings, empty states, dates, actions, confirmation dialogs and feedback subjects follow the selected English, Sinhala or Tamil interface language. Open **AI Matches** to see side-by-side reports, dimensions, confidence and cautions. Choose Confirm only when the reports plausibly relate; choose Not My Item otherwise. Corrections are reviewed by administrators before dataset use.

## 8. Claims
A claim uses five translated steps in English, Sinhala or Tamil: confirm the target; describe ownership; upload private evidence; answer item-specific questions; review/submit. Evidence is visible only to authorised participants/admins. AI does not approve claims. Lost/found detail pages and My Claims use the selected interface language for protected-contact explanations, handover confirmation, claim review and resolution actions. Match explanations, correction controls, claim cards and notification actions use the selected English, Sinhala or Tamil interface language; user-entered evidence and provider-generated explanations remain unchanged.

## 9. Contact and handover
Contact details appear only after the approved workflow or explicit public setting. Meet at a safe approved place. Do not mark received/handed-over until the physical exchange is complete. Both parties may need to confirm. When an exchange does not happen, use the translated cancellation confirmation and enter a short factual reason; the paired reports reopen for human review. Post-recovery feedback is separate from ownership evidence and must not include phone numbers, passwords or private claim material.

## 10. Notifications and status
Dashboard attention cards show matches, claims and pending handovers. Optional profile completion, app installation, push setup and iOS instructions follow the selected English, Sinhala or Tamil interface language and do not alter core reporting access without explicit user action. Workflow timelines show Submitted/Review/Contact/Handover/Completed. Open the trilingual **Notifications** centre to control optional push and non-security workflow email delivery by category: matches, claims, handover, reminders and general system updates. In-app workflow records remain available for safety and audit. Account verification, password reset and administrative security messages are not suppressed by these preferences.

## 11. Language and accessibility
Use the EN/සිං/தமிழ் switcher. Open the floating **Accessibility preferences** button to choose default, large or extra-large text; strengthen contrast; always reduce motion; or disable blur, glow and heavy shadows in low-effects mode. The settings apply immediately and stay only in the current browser until reset or browser storage is cleared. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, browser zoom and operating-system reduced-motion preferences remain supported. Report accessibility problems through the approved support channel.

## 12. Account deletion and support
Use account settings to request deletion. Active claims/legal holds may require resolution; historical records may be anonymised. For fraud, privacy or safety concerns contact **[approved support contact]**.

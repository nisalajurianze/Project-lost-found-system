# Terms of Use

> **Status:** University-review draft  
> **Release date:** 2026-07-26  
> **Important:** Replace bracketed institutional placeholders and obtain formal university approval before public deployment.

## Eligibility and acceptance

Use is limited to authorised university community members and approved administrators. By using the service, a user agrees to provide truthful information, protect credentials, respect privacy and follow university rules.

## Permitted use

Users may search public reports, create accurate lost/found reports, make good-faith ownership claims, upload relevant evidence, communicate through approved channels and confirm handover only after the physical exchange occurs.

## Prohibited conduct

Users must not submit false claims; impersonate another person; harvest contact/student data; upload unlawful, explicit, violent or unrelated material; expose passwords, full card numbers or private addresses; threaten or harass users; bypass access controls; scrape, probe or disrupt the service; automate claims; manipulate AI feedback; or use the platform to trade or sell property.

## Ownership and handover

A report, AI score, photograph or claim approval is not legal proof of ownership. The reporter and authorised university personnel must verify distinguishing details and complete a safe handover. Disputes are escalated to **[responsible university department]**.

## AI limitations

AI may be unavailable, inaccurate or incomplete. Users must review all generated text, categories, redactions, matches and questions. AI never automatically approves ownership, bans an account or makes a final disciplinary decision.

## Content moderation and account action

The university may hide content, request corrections, restrict features or suspend accounts where there is a documented safety, fraud or policy concern. High-risk flags are advisory; a human must review the evidence. Emergency action must be logged and appeal/escalation information provided where appropriate.

## Availability and liability

The service is provided to assist recovery and does not guarantee that an item will be found or returned. The university must approve final liability wording. Users remain responsible for safe meetings, accurate evidence and compliance with law.

## Termination, deletion and disputes

Users may request account deletion subject to active claims, legal holds and audit requirements. The service should anonymise historical records where deletion would break integrity. Complaints and appeals go to **[support/escalation contact]**.

## Governing policy

These terms are subject to Sri Lankan law and approved university regulations. Formal legal review is required before publication.

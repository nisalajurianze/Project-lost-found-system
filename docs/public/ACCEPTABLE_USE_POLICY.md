# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Status:** University-review draft  
> **Release date:** 2026-07-26  
> **Important:** Replace bracketed institutional placeholders and obtain formal university approval before public deployment.

## Required behaviour
- Create only genuine reports and claims.
- Use approximate public locations; keep private residences and room numbers out of public text.
- Upload only item/evidence images that you are authorised to share.
- Answer ownership questions truthfully and keep shared contact details confidential.
- Confirm handover only after both parties have physically completed it.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Fraudulent or repeated unrelated claims.
- Doxxing, harassment, threats, discriminatory content or retaliation.
- Credential sharing, account takeover, scraping or security testing without written authorisation.
- Uploading identity cards, payment cards, QR/payment data, passwords or unrelated personal documents.
- Manipulating report dates, locations, AI corrections or evidence to raise a score.
- Automated bulk submissions, spam or denial-of-service activity.

## Enforcement
Automated systems may flag suspicious activity but cannot impose a final sanction. An authorised person reviews evidence, records the decision and follows university disciplinary/escalation rules. Security emergencies may be temporarily contained before full review.

# Student and User Manual

## 1. Purpose

Use Smart Lost & Found to report a lost or found item, search existing reports, receive AI-assisted suggestions, submit a private claim, and confirm a safe handover.

## 2. Supported languages

The interface supports English, Sinhala, and Tamil. The chatbot also accepts natural Singlish and Tamilish. Language can be changed from the header. The chatbot may answer in the detected style; use its language selector when detection is wrong.

## 3. Create and secure an account

1. Open **Sign up**.
2. Enter your real name, institutional email, strong password, and required university identifier.
3. Complete email verification where enabled.
4. Never share passwords or verification/reset links.
5. Complete phone/student details before starting protected workflows.
6. Use **Profile** to review active details and delete/anonymise the account where permitted.

## 4. Search reports

1. Open **Search Items**, **Lost Items**, or **Found Items**.
2. Describe item, colour, brand, date, campus/village/road/landmark, or unique feature.
3. Apply type/category/status/date filters.
4. Review total result count, relevance reasons, and pagination/show-more.
5. Use the chatbot for conversational queries such as:
   - `black phone near library yesterday`
   - `mage nil bag eka engineering laga nathi una`
   - `library pakkathula wallet kidaichutha?`
6. AI relevance is not ownership proof.

## 5. Report a lost item

The four-step wizard provides:

1. **Photo & AI** - upload public item photos; AI suggestions appear for review and never silently overwrite fields.
2. **Item details** - name, category, brand/model, colours, material, description, unique marks, privacy-safe visible text, search tags.
3. **Place & time** - type a campus building, lane, village, shop, bus stop, or local nickname. A visible map is not required. Enter approximate time if exact time is unknown.
4. **Privacy & review** - choose contact visibility, review report-quality suggestions and possible duplicates, preview, and submit.

Draft text is saved locally; image files are not placed in local draft storage. Do not put full ownership secrets or personal IDs in public fields.

## 6. Report a found item

Use the same wizard and specify where the item is safely stored. Do not publish a private home/room location. Prefer an approved office, security desk, faculty reception, or other safe public handover point.

## 7. AI image analysis

AI may suggest item name, category/icon, description, tags, colours, brand/model, material, unique marks, and privacy-safe visible text. It may reject unrelated, blank, explicit, face/selfie, ID/card, or unsafe images. Apply/edit suggestions individually or continue manually.

## 8. Potential matches

A match card compares lost and found reports and explains category, text, location, date, colour, visual metadata, and other evidence. Confirming a suggestion only expresses interest; it does not establish ownership.

## 9. Submit a claim

1. Open the target report and choose **This might be mine** / equivalent action.
2. Describe private identifying information that was not fully public.
3. Upload private evidence only when useful.
4. Review AI-generated category-aware questions; answer honestly.
5. Submit and track status.

Private evidence is restricted to authorised workflow participants/admins. AI risk flags may prioritise human review but cannot automatically reject you.

## 10. Contact sharing and handover

The reporter/admin may share contact access after review. Arrange a safe handover, avoid isolated locations, and confirm only after physical transfer and identity/ownership checks. Both parties may need to complete confirmation.

## 11. Chatbot

The assistant can search all relevant reports, count/paginate results, refine earlier context, explain matches, answer workflow questions, show the user's reports/claims/matches when signed in, resolve verified local aliases, and ask one useful clarification when necessary.

Use feedback buttons for **Helpful**, **Incorrect**, or **Unsafe**. Never send passwords or full private proof in casual chat.

## 12. Teach the AI a location

Use **Teach AI a Location** for a missing lane, shop, bus stop, landmark, or local nickname. Submit the canonical/local name, area, aliases, and evidence. It remains unverified until admin review.

## 13. Notifications and PWA

Push notifications and installation are optional. Core workflows remain available without them. Browser/device restrictions may require installing the PWA before enabling push.

## 14. Accessibility

Use keyboard navigation, zoom, screen reader, reduced motion, and language controls. The space animation is decorative and becomes static/reduced when requested. Report barriers through the approved support channel; a manual alternative must be offered.

## 15. Safety and support

- Report fraudulent, unsafe, or privacy-invasive content.
- Do not confront a suspected claimant.
- For urgent safety/security matters, use official university emergency channels rather than the chatbot.
- If the system is unavailable, contact the approved physical lost-property office.
